## How to use this view

Start with a business process and an outcome. Identify the capabilities needed, record current evidence, then prioritise gaps. All five operating layers consume Knowledge & Memory, Trust & Control, and Workforce & Ways of Working.

## Planned content

A worked design, implementation alternatives, failure scenarios and a measurement guide. Detailed coverage is intentionally visible as unfinished; the map does not imply a complete implementation.

## Digitisation readiness

For each target process, establish:

1. A digital case or transaction identifier and accountable process owner.
2. An authoritative system for each business entity and status.
3. Recorded state transitions, decisions, exceptions and human approvals.
4. Supported read and write interfaces with access control and recovery behaviour.
5. Usable data quality, lineage, freshness and retention policies.
6. A path for customer or employee interactions to become tracked work.

ERP implementation, CRM adoption and channel digitisation are enabling business changes in their own right. A data lake cannot substitute for a missing operational process or an unowned system of record.
