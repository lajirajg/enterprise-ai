## Maintenance rhythm

Capture a URL immediately; classify it during review. Review new candidates regularly. Revisit curated links and capability coverage quarterly. Retire outdated resources with a reason instead of silently deleting their history. Prefer a small useful addition over reorganising the entire repository.
