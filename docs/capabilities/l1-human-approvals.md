# Human approval experience

Status: **In progress** — capability definition and acceptance example available; implementation comparison planned.

## Purpose

Let an authorised person inspect the proposed action, evidence and consequences.

Canonical ID: `l1-human-approvals` · Architecture area: [User Experience](../architecture/layers/user-experience/index.md)

## Evidence of capability

Record approver, scope and expiry; reject a stale or unauthorised approval.

Record the test case, expected result, observed result, service owner and evidence location. A product label alone is not evidence of coverage.
